# Editorial Perspective & Signature Themes

Ihnatko evaluates technology primarily through creative empowerment, user agency, and long-term value rather than raw benchmarks. In [Apple Ecosystem](../topics/apple-ecosystem.md) debates, he champions repairability, clear product positioning, and user-respecting software design, while pushing back on walled-garden overreach and subscription fatigue. In [AI and Machine Learning](../topics/ai-and-machine-learning.md) and [Tech Policy and Media](../topics/tech-policy-and-media.md), he frequently raises questions around creative copyright, human authorship, and how generative tools alter writing and media industries.[^archive-data]

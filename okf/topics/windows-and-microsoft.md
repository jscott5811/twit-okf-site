# Historical Arc & Year-by-Year Trajectory

Windows and Microsoft carries one of the deepest historical baselines in the archive, with 20,064 official topic keyword mentions recorded prior to 2020 across legacy episodes of [Windows Weekly](../shows/ww.md), [Security Now](../shows/sn.md), and [This Week in Tech](../shows/twit.md).[^archive-data] Consistent with thinner archive transcript ingestion during 2020–2021, dated official counts dipped to 439 in 2020 and 1,011 in 2021 before surging to 9,793 in 2022 as full weekly transcription resumed during Windows 11's first full year in market.[^archive-data][^dataset-doc]

# Cross-Show Editorial Coverage & Key Voices

[Windows Weekly](../shows/ww.md) provides the institutional memory for Microsoft coverage across the TWiT archive, anchored by [Leo Laporte](../people/leo-laporte.md) (with [Mikah Sargent](../people/mikah-sargent.md) stepping in as guest host) and longtime Microsoft chronicler [Paul Thurrott](../people/paul-thurrott.md).[^archive-data] Across the archive's highest-signal episodes, listeners witness an important co-host transition: veteran enterprise reporter Mary Jo Foley co-anchored through late 2022 (bringing deep Microsoft 365, Azure, and internal roadmap sourcing) before [Richard Campbell](../people/richard-campbell.md) joined Thurrott in 2023 to pair developer, cloud, and business-model perspective with Thurrott's famously candid critiques of Windows UI inconsistencies and Surface product decisions.[^archive-data]

Beyond Windows Weekly, [Hands-On Windows](../shows/how.md) delivers focused how-to walkthroughs for Windows 11 users, [Security Now](../shows/sn.md) dissects Patch Tuesday vulnerabilities, BitLocker key escrow, and Windows Recall security architecture, and [This Week in Tech](../shows/twit.md) frames Microsoft's cloud, gaming, and Copilot+ PC strategy against Apple and Google.[^archive-data]

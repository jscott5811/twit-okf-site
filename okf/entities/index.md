# Entities

* [Apple](apple.md) - Consumer hardware, operating system, and services giant whose ecosystem is the most frequently discussed vendor across all 17 TWiT shows.
* [Microsoft](microsoft.md) - Enterprise software, Windows operating system, Azure cloud, Copilot AI, and Xbox gaming platform tracked extensively across the archive.
* [Google](google.md) - Search, Android, Chrome, YouTube, Cloud, and Gemini AI platform spanning all 17 shows in the TWiT transcript archive.
* [OpenAI](openai.md) - Leading AI research lab behind ChatGPT, GPT models, and Sora, accounting for a major share of 2025–2026 TWiT discussions.
* [Anthropic](anthropic.md) - AI safety and frontier model company behind Claude, Claude Code, and the Model Context Protocol (MCP), surging sharply in 2025–2026.
* [NVIDIA](nvidia.md) - GPU and AI accelerator leader central to 2025–2026 discussions of AI data centers, semiconductors, PC gaming, and Linux graphics drivers.
* [Linux and FOSS Ecosystem](linux-and-foss.md) - Open-source kernel, distributions, desktop environments, and free software foundations tracked across 16 of 17 TWiT shows.
* [NASA and SpaceX](nasa-and-spacex.md) - Civil and commercial spaceflight leaders anchoring Mars exploration, Artemis lunar missions, Starship launches, and ISS operations.
* [GRC and Security Tools](grc-and-security-tools.md) - Gibson Research Corporation (SpinRite, ShieldsUP) and widely recommended security utilities (Bitwarden, Signal, Passkeys, WireGuard) across the archive.
* [Mozilla and the Open Web](mozilla-and-open-web.md) - Mozilla Foundation, Firefox browser, W3C/IETF web standards, and open-web advocacy across browser privacy and ad-blocking debates.
* [EFF and Digital Rights](eff-and-digital-rights.md) - Electronic Frontier Foundation, Internet Archive, CISA, and digital civil-liberties advocacy spanning encryption, copyright, and privacy.
* [Meta and Social Platforms](meta-and-social-platforms.md) - Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, Quest, Ray-Ban smart glasses) alongside decentralized and rival platforms (Bluesky, Mastodon, X, TikTok).

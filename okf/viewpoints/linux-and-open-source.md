# Key Fault Lines & Consensus Areas

- **"Year of the Linux Desktop" readiness:** [Jonathan Bennett](../people/jonathan-bennett.md) and [Leo Laporte](../people/leo-laporte.md) note that modern Fedora, Ubuntu, KDE/GNOME, and SteamOS setups are dramatically smoother than a decade ago, while Windows/Mac regulars point to lingering edge cases in pro creative apps and peripheral drivers.
- **Permissive vs. copyleft & source-available relicensing:** Open-source commentators frequently debate corporate relicensing of previously open projects and how maintainers can sustainably fund critical infrastructure.
- **Consensus area:** Broad recognition that open-source software powers modern cloud, AI, and security infrastructure—and that self-hosting (Docker, Home Assistant, Raspberry Pi) offers unmatched user control.[^archive-data]

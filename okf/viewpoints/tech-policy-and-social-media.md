# Key Fault Lines & Consensus Areas

- **Regulatory intervention vs. unintended speech/innovation harms:** [Paris Martineau](../people/paris-martineau.md) and [Cory Doctorow](../people/cory-doctorow.md) favor aggressive structural antitrust enforcement and interoperability mandates, while [Jeff Jarvis](../people/jeff-jarvis.md) warns against heavy-handed content regulation, age-verification mandates, and copyright overreach that could chill the open web.
- **Decentralized social media viability:** [Leo Laporte](../people/leo-laporte.md) and [Jeff Jarvis](../people/jeff-jarvis.md) enthusiastically experiment with Mastodon and Bluesky, while other panelists debate whether decentralized protocols can achieve mainstream network effects against Meta's Threads, TikTok, and X.
- **Consensus area:** Broad agreement that social media's ad-driven engagement incentives and opaque algorithmic feeds have eroded public trust, and that tariffs introduce real consumer-hardware price pressure.[^archive-data]

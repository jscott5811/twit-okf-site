# Key Fault Lines & Consensus Areas

- **Iterative commercial testing vs. traditional NASA risk aversion:** [Rod Pyle](../people/rod-pyle.md) and [Tariq Malik](../people/tariq-malik.md) regularly contrast SpaceX's hardware-rich, fail-fast Starship iteration against legacy cost-plus contracting (SLS, Starliner), noting both the speed benefits and regulatory/safety tensions.
- **Moon-first (Artemis) vs. Mars-direct ambitions:** Hosts debate whether sustained lunar infrastructure is an essential stepping stone or a distraction from Mars missions, especially amid shifting federal budgets.
- **Consensus area:** Strong admiration for robotic science missions (Mars rovers, James Webb, Europa Clipper) and agreement that reusable launch vehicles have permanently lowered the cost of access to orbit.[^archive-data]

**Notable People** is the master biographical index of the 20 most influential voices in the [TWiT Transcript Archive](../datasets/twit-transcript-archive.md), spanning both the network's core on-air hosts and regular panelists (15 profiles) and the most frequently cited external journalists, security researchers, and industry analysts (5 profiles).[^archive-data][^dataset-doc] Every metric on this page is computed strictly from the **3,350 official speaker-attributed episodes** in `data/` (99.1% named speaker attribution), excluding the 28 unattributed YouTube caption backfills in `data_youtube/` so that speaker episode counts and verbatim attribution remain exact.[^archive-data][^dataset-doc]

Together, these 20 individuals define the editorial backbone of TWiT's two-decade record (2005–2026).[^archive-data] Network founder [Leo Laporte](../people/leo-laporte.md) anchors the entire archive with **2,370 speaker episodes across all 17 active shows** (representing more than 70% of the official corpus), while [Steve Gibson](../people/steve-gibson.md) pairs **1,150 speaker episodes** with an archive-leading **6,732 spoken body mentions across all 17 shows**, reflecting the unique longevity of [Security Now](../shows/sn.md) and the network-wide reach of GRC security tools.[^archive-data]

A second structural pattern revealed by the index is the distinction between **network-wide utility hosts** and **deep domain specialists**.[^archive-data] [Mikah Sargent](../people/mikah-sargent.md) (564 episodes across 14 shows) and [Jason Howell](../people/jason-howell.md) (304 episodes across 11 shows) bridge nearly every show on the schedule, whereas specialists like [Tariq Malik](../people/tariq-malik.md) (113 episodes on [This Week in Space](../shows/twis.md)), [Rosemary Orchard](../people/rosemary-orchard.md) (150 episodes across 2 Apple-focused shows), and [Paul Thurrott](../people/paul-thurrott.md) (386 episodes anchored by [Windows Weekly](../shows/ww.md)) concentrate deep vertical expertise into flagship beat programs.[^archive-data]

# Methodology & Attribution Caveats

Speaker episode counts measure distinct official episodes in `data/` in which the individual appears as a named speaker tag (`- <Speaker> -`), whereas spoken body mentions count references to the person inside the spoken dialogue of any show.[^archive-data] Because YouTube auto-captions in `data_youtube/` lack speaker diarization, all 28 backfill files are omitted from this reference to preserve strict attribution accuracy.[^dataset-doc] Note also that active spans reflect available official transcripts (for instance, 2005–2013 is dominated by [Security Now](../shows/sn.md)), so earlier off-transcript guest appearances on other shows are not counted here.[^archive-data]
